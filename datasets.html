Dataset card

What a dataset entry documents.

In addition to the six common fields, every dataset entry answers the following.

Spatial coverage & unit
Extent, the spatial unit or raster resolution, and the coordinate reference system.
Time span & cadence
Period covered, observation dates, and whether and how the dataset is updated.
Variables & schema
Every field with its type, unit, allowed values, and missing-value convention.
Source & collection
Original providers, collection method, sampling, and the rights attached to each source.
Processing
Cleaning, joining, aggregation, and derivation steps, with the code that performs them.
Validation & quality
Completeness, consistency checks, comparison with reference data, and known error rates.
Limitations
Coverage gaps, biases, temporal mismatches, and uses the dataset does not support.
Artifacts & citation
Download or access route, format, version, licence, and how to cite the dataset.
