Follow UrbanProbe.

Choose what you want to hear about, then how you want to receive it. Nothing here needs an account on this site.

How it works

Each item has a Follow button. It opens a short list of ways to receive that item: email, a feed reader, or GitHub. The raw feed address is also shown, in case the button does not work.

What you can follow

Choose what to follow.

UrbanProbe is a registry. A new item appears when an entry is released, updated or deprecated, so the feeds are quiet most of the time. If a feed is empty today, follow it anyway: the first item will reach you when it appears.

One entry

Every entry page has its own Follow button, next to the buttons at the top of the page, named “Follow this dataset”, “Follow this indicator” and so on. It follows that entry only: new versions, a deprecation notice if there is one, and for benchmarks every new leaderboard result. Open any dataset, indicator or method in a registry to find it.

How the channels work

Four ways to receive updates.

  • Email: Blogtrottr, a free third-party service, can email you each new item. It shows ads, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

  • Feed reader: apps such as Feedly collect updates from many sites in one place. Copy the address and add it to your reader.

  • Calendar: subscribe once and new events appear in your calendar by themselves. Google Calendar can take up to a day to show changes.

  • GitHub: if you have a GitHub account, open the repository, choose Watch, then Custom, and tick Releases. GitHub then notifies you of every new release.

UrbanProbe has no calendar of its own. The events and deadlines calendars are on City Computing Collective.

Stop following

You can leave at any time.

Each service keeps its own list, so you stop in the same place where you started.

  • Email: every email has an unsubscribe link. Use it.

  • Feed reader: remove the feed from your reader.

  • Calendar: remove the calendar from your calendar app.

  • GitHub: open the repository, choose Watch, and choose Unwatch.

This site does not store anything about you. Email and reader services are run by the companies named here.
