Defined indicators.

Spatial indicators with explicit definitions, computation steps, validation, and sensitivity. An indicator enters this registry when it states what it measures and how well it does so.

Resource standard

Six common fields plus the indicator-specific fields below. Every indicator links to the datasets and method it is computed from.

What an indicator entry documents.

Every indicator entry answers the following. The name in code is the key used in the registry data and in the proposal form.

Definition & rationale definition
What the indicator is meant to measure, the concept behind it, and why this operationalisation.
Inputs provenance
The datasets it is computed from, with versions, and any parameters or thresholds used.
Computation processing
The formula or algorithm, step by step, with the code that implements it.
Spatial unit & scale spatial_scale
The unit the indicator is reported at, and how it behaves when aggregated or disaggregated.
Coverage & time geography_time
Places and periods for which values are released.
Validation validation
Agreement with ground truth, surveys, or established measures, and the results of those comparisons.
Sensitivity & uncertainty sensitivity
How the indicator responds to parameter choices and input errors, and how uncertainty is reported.
Limitations & misuse limitations
Contexts where the indicator is not valid, and interpretations it does not support.
Licence, access & citation version license access links citation
Computed values, code, version, licence, and citation.
