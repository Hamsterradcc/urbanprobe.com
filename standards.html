Resource standard · v1

What it takes to enter a registry.

One standard, three registries. The common fields apply to every resource; each registry adds the fields its kind needs. Missing fields block a release.

How the standard is used

Resource cards are stored as JSON in the repository and rendered on the registry pages. Changes to the standard are versioned and recorded in the repository history.

Common fields

Required for every resource.

Geography & time
Spatial extent, spatial unit or resolution, time span, and update cadence.
Provenance
Original sources, how they were obtained, and the rights attached to them.
Processing
Every step between the source and the released resource, with the code that performs it.
Licence & access
The licence of the release, any access conditions, and what may not be redistributed.
Validation
How the resource was checked, against what, and the results of those checks.
Limitations & citation
Known gaps, biases, and misuse risks, with a version and how to cite the resource.
Registry 01

Dataset fields.

Spatial coverage & unitExtent, the spatial unit or raster resolution, and the coordinate reference system.
Time span & cadencePeriod covered, observation dates, and whether and how the dataset is updated.
Variables & schemaEvery field with its type, unit, allowed values, and missing-value convention.
Source & collectionOriginal providers, collection method, sampling, and the rights attached to each source.
Validation & qualityCompleteness, consistency checks, comparison with reference data, and known error rates.
ArtifactsDownload or access route, format, version, licence, and citation.
Registry 02

Indicator fields.

Definition & rationaleWhat the indicator is meant to measure, the concept behind it, and why this operationalisation.
InputsThe datasets it is computed from, with versions, and any parameters or thresholds used.
ComputationThe formula or algorithm, step by step, with the code that implements it.
Spatial unit & scaleThe unit the indicator is reported at, and how it behaves when aggregated or disaggregated.
Sensitivity & uncertaintyHow the indicator responds to parameter choices and input errors, and how uncertainty is reported.
ArtifactsComputed values, code, version, licence, and citation.
Registry 03

Method fields.

Problem & intended usersThe urban question the method answers and who is expected to run it.
Inputs & outputsRequired inputs with formats and sources, and the outputs produced with their schema.
Steps & dependenciesThe pipeline in order, with software dependencies and their versions.
Installation & runtimeHow to set up the environment, expected run time, and hardware requirements.
Example & test dataA worked example with small test data that reproduces a published result.
Maintenance statusWhether the method is maintained, who maintains it, and known open issues.
ArtifactsRepository, release version, licence, and citation.
Release checklist

Before a resource goes live.

Card completeEvery required field is filled. “To follow” is not an accepted value.
RegenerableThe processing code runs from the documented sources and reproduces the released resource.
Rights checkedSource licences permit the release; restricted or personal data is described, not redistributed.
Version assignedThe resource carries a version, a release date, and a citation; later changes get a new version.
Status & versioning

How resources change over time.

draftCard in preparation. Stored in the repository but not shown on the registry pages.
releasedCard complete and artifacts public. Shown on the registry page with its version.
deprecatedSuperseded or withdrawn. Remains listed for the record, with a pointer to its replacement where one exists.
VersionsSemantic versions. A change to sources, processing, definition, or schema increments the version; downstream resources state which version they used.
Out of scope

What is not accepted.

Download links without documentationFiles with no provenance, processing history, or licence.
Indicators without a definitionComputed values with no statement of what they measure or how they were validated.
Methods that do not runCode without an environment, example, or output that can be checked.
Restricted or personal dataMaterial the submitter does not have the right to release, or that identifies individuals.
Propose a resource

Open an issue with the card attached.

Proposals and data requests are reviewed against this standard in the public repository. Corrections to existing resources follow the same route.
